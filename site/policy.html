Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Relay is self-hosted software. This policy explains how your data is handled, which is different from a normal app because there is no central service run by the developer.

The short version. Relay stores your data only on the server that you or your host runs. The developer of Relay operates no servers, receives none of your data and cannot see your library, your account or your activity. Everyone who signs in to the same server can see the shared content on it.

Who runs the service

Relay is a client for a server that you host. When you use Relay you connect to a server address that you enter yourself. That server is operated by you or by whoever set it up for you. It is not operated by the developer of the app. There is no shared Relay account and no central database.

What data exists and where it lives

All data stays on the server you connect to. That includes:

  • Your account, meaning your username and your password stored as a secure hash.
  • Your music library, its tags and cover art.
  • Your playlists, your Liked Songs and your listening history.
  • Your profile and presence, meaning whether you are online and what you are playing.

None of this is transmitted to the developer or to any Relay operated service, because none exists. If you stop using your server and delete it, that data is gone.

What other people on your server can see

Relay is designed for a shared server used by a small group. On a given server:

  • The music library is shared. Everyone signed in to that server can browse and play the same tracks.
  • Profiles and activity are visible to other users on the same server, including whether you are online and what you are playing or last played.
  • Public playlists and playlists you collaborate on are visible to the people they are shared with.
Only join or invite people you trust. Because content is shared within a server, anyone with an account on that same server can see the shared library and your activity on it. Treat a Relay server like a shared room, not a private vault.

Data the app sends off your server

The app itself does not send your personal data to third parties. Two things are worth knowing:

  • Your server may fetch lyrics and translations from external services when music is added, so that lyrics can be shown while you listen. This is a request made by your server, using the track information, not your personal data.
  • Streaming audio and cover art travels between your device and your own server over an encrypted connection.

There are no advertising networks, no analytics trackers and no third-party data brokers in the app.

Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes, never in plain text. Access uses short lived tokens. A typical deployment sits behind a tunnel so the server is not directly exposed on the public internet. Login attempts are rate limited. The security of your specific server also depends on how you configure and run it.

Your control

Because you run the server, you are in control of your data. You can delete your account, remove tracks or shut down the server entirely. When the server is gone, so is the data on it. There is nothing held elsewhere to request or delete.

Children

Relay is a general purpose media player for content you host. It is not directed at children and collects no data centrally.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
